# Examples

These show the *level* we want, not templates. Never copy their sentences, structure or
openings: every real post has to find its own shape. The facts in them are illustrative only.

---

## Good

**National day, image of the flag on a building at dusk, 64 words**

> Fifty-five years ago this week, six emirates signed up to a shared future, and Ras Al Khaimah
> joined a few weeks later. Most of the people who live here today weren't born yet, and many of
> us came from somewhere else entirely.
>
> That's the part we find remarkable: a country that keeps making room.
>
> Eid Al Etihad Mubarak to everyone who calls the UAE home. 🇦🇪
>
> #EidAlEtihad #UAE

Why it works: a specific number, a true observation (and an accurate one: six emirates formed
the union on 2 December 1971; Ras Al Khaimah joined in February 1972), warmth without slogans,
ends cleanly.

---

**Condolence, text only, 41 words**

> Nakoda AI extends its sincere condolences to the family of [full name and title as WAM
> writes it], and to the people of the UAE.
>
> May Allah have mercy on him and grant his family patience and solace.

Why it works: prompt, plain, nothing else in it.

---

**Happy news, official handout photo, 96 words**

> The Rashid rover's team has been waiting for this photo for months.
>
> The Mohammed bin Rashid Space Centre released the first images this morning, taken [detail
> from the source]. Engineers in Dubai built most of the hardware themselves, which is the
> detail worth sitting with: ten years ago the UAE was buying satellites, not designing them.
>
> Congratulations to the MBRSC team.

Why it works: opens on a human detail, one concrete fact, one honest perspective, congratulates
by name.

---

**News with a perspective, text only, 148 words**

> Dubai's new rules on AI in government services take effect on 1 January, and the part most
> companies will feel first isn't the headline one.
>
> Every entity using AI to make or support a decision about a person will need to be able to
> explain that decision in plain language, on request, within [timeframe from the source], the
> Digital Dubai notice says.
>
> That's easy to agree with and hard to do. Most deployed models were never built to explain
> themselves, and many vendors can't say what their system was trained on.
>
> The organisations that will be ready are the ones that start with an inventory: which
> decisions, which systems, who owns them. That's a spreadsheet, not a project, and it's a good
> first week of December.

Why it works: specific source, a real "so what", practical, no sales pitch, no question at the
end.

## Bad (and why)

> 🚀 Exciting times! In today's rapidly evolving AI landscape, the UAE continues to be a beacon
> of innovation. 💡 This is not just about technology — it's about transforming lives. What do
> you think? 👇 #AI #Innovation #UAE #Dubai #FutureOfWork #Leadership #Tech

Every tell at once: hype emoji, landscape, beacon, not-just-it's-about, engagement bait,
hashtag wall, no actual information.

> Happy National Day! 🇦🇪🇦🇪🇦🇪 Wishing everyone a wonderful celebration. Proud to be part of this
> amazing nation's journey!

Generic: could be any company, any year. Nothing true or specific in it.
